# Code of conduct

Aether HF is built by radio amateurs for radio amateurs, and the standard is the one the
hobby already has: be excellent to each other, on and off the air.

## What that means here

* **Technical disagreement is welcome.** Argue with measurements, curves, references and
  recordings. "This decodes at −10 dB on my bench and here is the WAV" is the kind of
  disagreement this project runs on.
* **People are not the argument.** No personal attacks, no harassment, no put-downs about
  someone's licence class, experience, equipment, country or employer, and no comments
  about anyone's identity. Gatekeeping ("you should not be doing this until you…") is not
  review.
* **Newcomers are the point.** Many of the people who will use this are new to HF data,
  to Git or to both. A question that seems basic gets a plain answer or a link, never a
  sigh.
* **Say what you did, not what you claim.** A performance figure comes with the benchmark
  behind it; a field report says what was measured and what was only seen.
* **Respect the law and the band.** Discussion of encrypting amateur traffic, of
  operating outside a licence, or of interfering with other users of a frequency has no
  place here.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages), on
any channel the project runs, and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (KK4ODA) may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block contributors who ignore this, with or without warning depending on what was
done. Report a problem privately to the maintainer's e-mail on the repository profile, or
through a GitHub private report if it concerns them. Reports are handled in confidence.
