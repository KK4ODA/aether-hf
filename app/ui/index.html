Disconnect finishes sending what is queued and then closes. Abort does not. Beacon puts this station's callsign on the air once, addressed to nobody, so anyone listening can report hearing you.

Keying and drive

−12 dB

Key puts the radio into transmit with no audio: watch the rig and the interface's PTT light. Tune keys with a steady tone at the transmit level; the level follows the slider while it plays, so set the rig's ALC by ear and eye, then press the button again to stop. Both are refused during a session, and a tone is refused while the channel is busy. The level is saved when the slider is released.

A recording is what the radio delivered, at 48 kHz, and a file beside it saying what the modem made of it — every frame, its SNR, whether it decoded. It is what a bug report about the air should carry, and what field/ keeps.
