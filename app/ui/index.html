Disconnect finishes sending what is queued and then closes. Abort does not. Beacon puts this station's callsign on the air once, addressed to nobody, so anyone listening can report hearing you.

A recording is what the radio delivered, at 48 kHz, and a file beside it saying what the modem made of it — every frame, its SNR, whether it decoded. It is what a bug report about the air should carry, and what field/ keeps.
